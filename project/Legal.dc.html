This Privacy Policy explains how Flykraft Synergies Private Limited, operating as Off Stage Productions ("Off Stage", "we", "us"), collects, uses, shares and protects personal data when you visit our website, contact us, or engage us for events and productions. We process personal data in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under them.

1. Information we collect

Details you give us: name, email address, phone number, company name, event date, venue, budget, guest numbers and any other information you share in enquiries, briefs, calls or messages. Booking and billing details: billing name and address, GST number and payment references. We do not store card or bank credentials; payments are handled by banks or payment gateways. Automatically collected data: IP address, device and browser type, pages visited and time spent, through cookies and similar technologies (see our Cookie Policy). Event media: photographs and video recorded at events we produce, which may include attendees.

2. How we use it

To respond to enquiries and prepare proposals and quotations. To plan, produce and deliver events, and coordinate with venues, vendors and crew. To issue invoices, collect payments and meet tax and accounting obligations. To improve our website and services. To send updates about our work, only where you have agreed; you can opt out at any time. To meet legal obligations and protect our rights.

3. Consent and legitimate uses

We process personal data with your consent, or for legitimate uses permitted by law, such as performing a contract you have asked us to enter into. You may withdraw consent at any time by writing to us. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out, and may limit our ability to deliver services.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share it only as needed with venues, vendors, technical crew and subcontractors engaged for your event; with service providers for hosting, email, analytics, payments and accounting; and with government or law-enforcement authorities where required by law. Everyone we share data with is expected to protect it and use it only for the stated purpose.

5. Event photography

We may use photos and videos from events we produce in our portfolio, website and social media, including @offstage.pr, unless the client asks us not to in writing. If you appear in our content and want it removed, email us and we will act promptly.

6. Retention and security

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by tax, accounting and other laws, then delete or anonymise it. We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Your rights

You may ask to access a summary of your personal data, correct or update it, have it erased where it is no longer needed, withdraw consent, and nominate another person to exercise your rights. You may also raise a grievance with us, and with the Data Protection Board of India if it is not resolved.

8. Children

Our website and services are not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect their personal data without verifiable parental consent.

9. Grievance officer

For questions, requests or complaints about your personal data, contact our Grievance Officer at hello@offstagepr.in or +91 73700 02080. We aim to acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and resolve them within 15 days.

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page with its revision date.

This policy explains how cancellations, postponements and refunds work for services booked with Off Stage Productions, a brand of Flykraft Synergies Private Limited. Events need crew, vendors and equipment committed well in advance, so the timing of a cancellation affects what can be refunded. If a signed Proposal has different terms, those terms apply.

1. Booking advance

The booking advance secures your date and our team, and covers early planning and creative work. It is non-refundable. It can be moved to a new date under section 3.

2. Cancellation by the client

Cancellations must be sent in writing to hello@offstagepr.in. Charges are based on how long before the event date we receive the notice:

More than 60 daysRefund of amounts paid beyond the booking advance, less non-recoverable vendor costs
30–60 days50% of the total fee is payable or retained
15–29 days75% of the total fee is payable or retained
Less than 15 days100% of the total fee is payable; no refund

In every case, costs already spent or committed to third parties are not refundable. These include venue deposits, custom fabrication, printing, travel, artist fees and rentals.

3. Postponement

If you postpone with at least 30 days' notice, we will carry amounts paid over to a new date within 6 months, subject to availability. Costs caused by the change in date may be charged extra. A second postponement, or one with less than 30 days' notice, is treated as a cancellation.

4. Cancellation by us

If we cancel for reasons within our control, we will refund all amounts paid, including the booking advance.

5. Force majeure

If an event cannot go ahead because of a force majeure event, as defined in our Terms & Conditions, we will first offer a new date. If that is not possible, we will refund amounts paid, minus work already done and costs committed to third parties.

6. Changes in scope

If you reduce the scope after confirmation, the fees may be revised. Charges for elements already produced or committed still apply.

7. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are processed within 10–14 working days to the original payment method or the bank account of the paying party. Applicable taxes are adjusted as required by law. Bank or gateway charges, if any, may be deducted.
